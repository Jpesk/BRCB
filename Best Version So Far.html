Step-by-Step Instructions
  1. Clear & sanitize prep space.
  2. Turn burner on:
    • Plug into power strip.
    • Place pot on burner (metal must be detected to heat up).
    • Press ⏻ (ON/OFF) button.
    • Press ⛭ (SELECT) → “1150 W” should be displayed on the screen & burner should begin to warm up.
  3. Add Wet Ingredients To Pot: 4 Cups Water + 4 Cups Sugar.
  4. Add Dry Ingredients to Pot: 2 Cups Lucky Charms.
  5. Bring to a rolling boil; stir until sugar & marshmallows are dissolved.
  6. Turn off burner & unplug.
  7. Strain the syrup into a pitcher to remove remaining cereal pieces.
    NOTE: Let the pot cool a bit before straining.
  8. Let syrup cool in pitcher for 1 hour.
  9. Use funnel to fill the glass Monin bottles with syrup & date for +2 weeks.

2. Brewing & 20L Bucket Prep
  1. Put the mesh bag into the cloth bag.
  2. Set Grinder to Coarsest setting.
  3. Grind 5lb Cold Brew blend into mesh/cloth bags.
  4. Prep 20L bucket: Put the finished ground bag into the 20L. Do your best to fit the bag around the lip of the bucket.
  5. Slowly pour 2 gal. water in a circular formation over the grounds to bloom the coffee. (You should be able to see bubbles forming on the top!)
  6. Zip-tie the mesh bag, then tie the cloth bag.
  7. Try to fit 3 gal. of water outside the bag. Do not go past the top part of the handle (the lid will not fit without spilling).
  8. Finalize & Store in BOH Fridge: Put date dots 1–3 on cold brew lid and date dot 4 on the POS system to inform staff when the kegging process should begin. Finalize & Submit Brewing Log.

COLD BREW: KEGGING PROCESS
Drip Drain • Digital Refractometer (1.9–2.1 TDS) • Kegging, Gas Purge & Cleanup
Materials Needed
iPad • Cold Brew Concentrate (CBC) • Cold Water • Wire Rack • Keg • Thermometer • Digital Refractometer (DR) • Distilled Water (Room Temp)
Reminders Before You Start
  • Be sure to check both batch numbers (POS and Lid of CBC).
  • Take the Distilled water out when you start so it can hit room temp.
1. Start Batchlog & Drip Drain Ground Bag
  1. Open Cold Brew Production & Batch Log from ConnectTeams → Select End of Brewing. Fill out: Brew Start time, Brew end time, Temperature of cold brew (make sure thermometer is calibrated beforehand — Should be below 41°F), and Cold brew roast.
  2. Using Gloves, remove the lid and pick up the cloth grounds bag: Set the wire rack on the CBC rim then set the bag on the rack to drip drain. (Note: If you are not drip draining, be careful when squeezing the cloth bag, do not place too much pressure on the wire rack because it is fragile.)
  3. When finished dripping, open the cloth bag & pull out the disposable ground bag and throw it in the trash.
  4. After removing the disposable bag there should be concentrated cold brew sitting in the bottom of the cloth bag — DO NOT use the remaining liquid in the cloth bag, dump down sink.
  5. Stir the concentrate with the Toddy Stick to mix in solids. Move on to TDS Testing.
2. Using the Digital Refractometer (Ideal Reading: 1.9 to 2.1)
⚠️ The D.R is used to help find the correct ratio of water to CBC. The D.R is also a baby and will break easily — please BE CAREFUL! DO NOT FLOOD THE D.R, USE ONLY 3 DROPS.
  1. Zero Calibration: Remove the D.R & Pipette from their case. Using pipette, drip 3x drops of room temperature distilled water onto D.R. Hit Menu (2x), then tap GO on “Set Zero?” to set the D.R. THOROUGHLY wipe D.R after reading.
  2. Initial TDS Reading: Fill pipette with CB, and put 3x drops onto D.R. Hit Menu until you see “Coffee % TDS”, then hit “GO”. Record this reading in the form (Initial TDS Reading). The concentrate on its own should read Above 3.0.
  3. Dilution: Using cold water, add approximately half of the water (4 qts) into the concentrate before stirring thoroughly.
  4. Repeat the pipette & D.R reading process again until cold brew is in TDS range — Ideal Reading is 1.9 to 2.1. If the number is too high, repeat the dilution process with half gallons, making sure to stir and use the D.R each time to get the best results.
3. Kegging & Purging
  1. The keg is very heavy—use a smaller pitcher to pour into the keg until comfortable pouring the CBC directly.
  2. Pour completed, tested cold brew into Keg. (Note: shimmy the keg back and forth to break up the foam so you can fit in as much as possible.)
  3. Once filled, take the temperature of the cold brew in the keg and record it into the form (Final Product Temp).
  4. Lid the Keg: Insert the lid at an angle for an easier time. Press down the handle to seal shut. Pull up on the lid/keg when inserted to ENSURE seal is well aligned.
  5. Purge the Keg: Remove the red lines (gas lines) from another keg. Connect the gas to the new keg for 2 minutes to purge the cold brew. While purging, put date dots on the new keg. Bring to BOH fridge FIFO’d for storage.
4. Cleaning Up
  • Cloth bag: Rinse with water and wring out until the water comes out clear. Immerse the bag in sanitizer for at least 2 minutes, then wring out and hang to dry.
  • Digital Refractometer: The pipette can be cleaned with sanitizer and the metal tray should be wiped off with a napkin after each use.
  • Keg: Wipe down from any spillage so it does not create a mess in fridges.
  • Cambro & wire rack: Wash normally in the triple sink.
🚫 NEVER PUT CLOTH BAG IN SOAPY WATER!
🚫 DO NOT SUBMERGE REFRACTOMETER!

1. Grinder Workflow (How to Grind)
  1. Put the portafilter in the X1 slot.
  2. Push the funnel down.
  3. Press hopper selection (if the beans in the basket icon is highlighted, it will preweigh).
  4. It will first weigh out the designated amount then it will start grinding — make sure to be patient.
  5. Wait till green portafilter is displayed on screen.
  6. Pull the grind knocker tab towards you at least 5 times.
  7. Push the funnel up.
  8. Tap the portafilter on the designated mat a couple of times (gently).
  9. Use the distributor to level out grinds all the way.
  10. Tamp! → Pull shot.
2. Dialing In
  1. Make sure to use the same head, same portafilter, and same button while dialing in.
  2. Set up your scale by setting the shot tin on it and taring it out (once on, press the power/mode button).
  3. Pull a shot and be sure to measure the input, output and ensure it is in servable range!
  4. If the shot is out of our servable window, Pull one more before adjusting grinder.
  5. Calculate the amount to modify the grind:
    • Too short (Below 25s): grind is too coarse → make grind more fine = decrease microns.
    • Too long (Above 30s): grind is too fine → make grind more coarse = increase microns.
    • TIP: A 2 micron change will +/- 1 second.
  6. To Adjust the grind, select the (Micron) number below the grey line. Adjust the top number (not the speed) up/down your calculation. Press the check mark to finalize.
  7. Pull a shot [With the same portafilter head as the first pull] to find the new time. If the first pull is not within range be sure to pull another shot before adjusting again. Repeat steps 2–6 until in window.
3. How to Change Roasts
Defiance (Left Hopper): Our standard and should be set up each morning to pre-weigh out beans for faster grinding. Defiance: 18 grams, around 180 microns.
Decaf (Right Hopper): Our standard and should be set up each morning to pre-weigh out beans for faster grinding. Decaf: 18 grams, around 175 microns.

Single Origins (Center By-Pass Hopper):

  1. There will be small airtight containers that will be pre-dosed and sealed.
  2. When needed, open the top compartment of the by-pass hopper (pull forward toward you), then dump the beans into the 3rd hopper.
  3. Once the lid goes back, select the middle chamber button (the basket that has the bottom highlighted).
  4. Grab the info sheet of the roasts and select the correct set (microns), and Speed (rpm). (The Memory 1 should be for Decaf — BUT Double check with info sheet.)
  5. Select the middle chamber button again and it will grind the beans in the hopper to use. Press back to exit menu.

X1 GRINDER — OPENING, CLOSING & TROUBLESHOOTING
Morning Setup • Nightly Hopper Steam-Clean • General Rules • Jam & Error Recovery
4. Opening Sequence
  1. Take off the by-pass hopper (black plastic X piece at the front of the machine).
  2. Place the hopper on and push back till it locks in place. Repeat for 2nd hopper.
  3. The blue microfiber rag will now be the day’s fresh sanitizer rag (be sure to rinse and wring out before use).
  4. Put the Defiance in the left hopper and the Single Origin in the right hopper. Then put on the by-pass hopper. Make sure only the first (out of three) dots are highlighted.
  5. Hold the name of the right roast and select the correct one that matches the single origin put into the hopper. Make sure that only the first dot is highlighted.
  6. Dial in each roast (make sure that you are doing one at a time, starting with Defiance).
  7. After dialing in, hold the bracket button underneath the roast selection (beans will appear in the bracket once selected) on the Defiance side. This will pre-weigh your beans for faster results.
5. Closing Sequence
  1. [15 minutes before close] Turn off pre-weight — Hold the bracket button with beans icon.
  2. Take off the by-pass hopper (the front, black plastic X1 piece) and pull the hopper straight towards you in order to take it off the grinder.
  3. Put the beans into an airtight container, and label the container with what roast it is. (If beans are stuck in the spiral in the hopper, twist it as you pour the beans out.)
  4. Use the steam wand on the inside of the hopper for around 10 seconds (till the inside is all foggy).
  5. Unscrew the white piece from the hopper and take out the spiral piece.
  6. Grab a new blue microfiber rag and wipe down the inside of the hopper and accessories.
  7. Hook the white piece back onto the spiral (with the side that looks bent).
  8. Put the two pieces into the hopper and secure with the white ring/washer. Do NOT put it tight — just to the point where it gently stops twisting.
  9. Leave the hopper upside down (on the blue microfiber rag that you wipe down the hopper with) overnight to dry.
  10. Set the black X plastic piece back onto the grinder for safe keeping. Repeat these steps with the other chamber.
  11. After or before the hoppers, be sure to lift up on the portafilter holder and it should come off. Brush all grinds from the holder and on the grinder. Then place and push down to put the holder back in place.
6. General Guidelines
🛑 Do NOT move the X1 Grinder. If it accidentally moves, please text the store manager immediately.
  • Make sure we are wiping it down (on all sides) at switchover and EOD.
  • Make sure to always be on top of filling the beans (try not to run out so it doesn't run into problems and/or break).
7. Problem Solving
1. Jammed (Most Common)
  1. Grab a wooden stir stick to put up into the funnel looking chamber and wiggle it around to loosen the grind.
  2. Afterwards, use the purge button (gear button in the middle of the screen).
  3. WARNING — please do not do ANYTHING while this is on.
  4. Press the button to turn on and then again to turn off.
2. Error Code Selecting Roast

If there is an error code, it most likely means that there is already a weighed out pre-dose in the chamber.

Fix: Press the selected chamber to grind it out. Then you can change the roast.

3. Flashing Hopper (Ran Out)
  1. Turn the machine off and then on again.
  2. Once booted up, the selections will then be red.
  3. Press the desired one and clear what is in the chamber. Dump the grind and it will be set for the next shot.
